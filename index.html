Research & Experience

Undergraduate Researcher

UNC Charlotte

Hemp Hurd Cellulose Extraction & Characterization

Faculty-advised research turning hemp hurd — the woody core left over from fiber processing — into a usable composite feedstock. I work across the full chain: extracting cellulose, compounding by twin-screw extrusion, injection molding ASTM specimens, and characterizing the result by FTIR, TGA, and SEM to see what the processing actually did to the material.

Twin-screw extrusion Injection molding ASTM specimen testing FTIR / TGA / SEM
